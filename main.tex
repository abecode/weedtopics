\section{Introduction}

This is a LaTeX package that provides labels for chapters, sections, subsections, and topic headings
that are "in the weeds", a.k.a. tangential, off-topic, or esoteric.  For example, if you were making 
a textbook, you may want students to skip certain sections unless they were curious.

Thanks to OpenAI Prism \LaTeX{} vibe-coding and research application.

Use \verb|\weedsection[1]{Title}|, \verb|\weedsection[2]{Title}|, or
\verb|\weedsection[3]{Title}| to mark tangential sections. The portable package
also provides \verb|\weedsubsection|, \verb|\weedchapter|, and
\verb|\weedspecial[<level>]{<topic>}|.

You can also use these one-argument shortcuts:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Sections: \verb|\weedysection|, \verb|\weediersection|, and \verb|\weediestsection|
  \item Subsections: \verb|\weedysubsection|, \verb|\weediersubsection|, and \verb|\weediestsubsection|
  \item Topics: \verb|\weedyspecial|, \verb|\weedierspecial|, and \verb|\weediestspecial|
\end{itemize}
